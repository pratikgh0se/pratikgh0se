### What I love building

- **Kubernetes controllers & operators in Go**: reconcile loops, CRDs, and control planes that keep clusters honest.
- **Infrastructure automation**: Terraform, ArgoCD, and CI/CD pipelines that turn runbooks into code.
- **Observability**: Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry, so systems explain themselves before anyone asks.

### At work

- **EKS migration**: led the move to Amazon EKS, saving **$720K/year**, with Terraform-automated backups and alerts.
- **Observability consolidation**: unified Prometheus, Thanos, and Elasticsearch onto one platform and cut daily data from **16TB to 4TB**.
- **Pharos (automated RCA)**: correlates metrics, logs, and tickets with retrieval-augmented generation; **MTTR down 20%**.
- **Hotfix automation**: a Slack-driven release flow that automated **80%** of the manual steps.
